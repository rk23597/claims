2 Protection scope

Which messages the policy protects. IN verifies the request; OUT signs the response; FAULT signs the error response.

3 JWS source

Where the serialized JWS is read (IN) and written (OUT/FAULT). MESSAGE_BODY is fixed to message.content.

4 Header validation

Protected-header checks. Pick a known claim from the list, or use + Custom claim for any other name. Reserved names (alg, kid, crit, b64, enc) are blocked. Tick crit to also add the name to crit.
